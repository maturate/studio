### sheldon_01

Episode: S4E23, The Agreement Dissection
Speakers: Sheldon, Priya, Leonard
Facet: rules and contracts

Context: Priya is Raj's sister, Leonard's girlfriend and a lawyer. She asks to read the Roommate Agreement Sheldon wrote. He hands it over certain it is airtight, and she takes it apart with ordinary contract law, then uses his own addendum against him. He goes from smug to stricken; "do so, do so" is a child losing a playground argument. Leonard gloats from the side. Sheldon storms off. The binder is his whole worldview as a prop, and this is the one time a colder rules-lawyer beats him with it.

[visual: Sheldon hands the binder over, proud of it]

Sheldon: Do so. Do so.
Priya: Oh, I'm afraid not. Section 7 here, on the right to enter the bathroom in emergency situations, is not specific as to what constitutes an emergency.
Sheldon: Oh, that's ridiculous. A bathroom emergency is self-explanatory.
Priya: Is it? If Leonard forgot to trim his nose hair, could he barge in while you were showering?
Sheldon: Irrelevant. Leonard doesn't trim his nose hair. He thinks because he's short, nobody can see up there.

[visual: Sheldon's face drops as the principle lands]

Sheldon: This isn't over.
[visual: Sheldon storms out]
Leonard: No offense, but shower sex with you is now the second-best thing that's happened today.
