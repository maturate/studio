## Part B. References

Six references, two per angle. Chatbot count per video follows the source clip.

### How each reference is built

Every scenario was sent to the live models and the real answers are recorded under the clip. The result picks the angle:

- **Angle A, the reference is fabricated.** The live answer differs from the clip. Concede the clip's best answer was good, then reveal what the model really said. The real chat on screen is the proof. No separate evidence step.
- **Angle B, the reference is genuine.** The live answer matches the clip. Concede it was good, then refuse the moral frame and go dark. superOS never claims on camera to have tested anything in this angle.
- **Angle C, deny the premise.** First say which answer in the room was the best, then go cocky. "Wouldn't have happened under superOS." Sits on top of A or B as a closing move; it does not depend on the test result.

Rules that hold across all six: never roast the best answer on merits. A good answer gets credit, even from a rival. Dark, rude, dismissive is fine. Putting words in a model's mouth is not. If a real model already made the point superOS wants to make, superOS cannot claim it.
