**Structure \-** 

1. Hook Opens with a curiosity-driven promise, positioning the tool as a useful WhatsApp discovery that viewers will want to see for themselves.  
2. Setup Introduces a simple website with a single button that takes users directly into a WhatsApp chat, making the process feel quick and frictionless.  
3. Transition A brief visual jump from the website to WhatsApp that delivers the reveal and smoothly moves into the demonstration.  
4. Demo 1 (Text Logging) Shows the most straightforward use case: sending a meal as a message and instantly receiving a structured nutritional breakdown.  
5. Demo 2 (Photo Logging) Highlights an even easier option by allowing users to send a photo of their food instead of typing everything out.  
6. Demo 3 (Asking Questions) Expands the use case beyond meal logging by showing how users can ask broader questions, such as checking their weekly progress.  
7. Payoff Reinforces the main benefit: everything happens inside WhatsApp, removing the need for a separate app or manual tracking.  
8. Closer Ends with a simple "Easy," giving the video a casual, confident finish that feels more like a recommendation than a pitch.

**Reference Trial Script \-**   
**dad won't have to keep reminding me about bills anymore**  
he sends it over, and honestly i usually forget until the last day  
this time i just sent a photo of it in the chat instead  
it took care of the rest, no typing anything in myself  
paid it in a couple taps, done before he even had to ask twice

**Structure \-** 

1. **Hook** Opens with a relatable problem many people have experienced: getting reminded about bills by their parents because they keep forgetting to pay them.  
2. **Setup** Establishes the user's habit of procrastinating bill payments until the last possible moment, creating a familiar pain point.  
3. **Discovery** Introduces a simpler alternative by showing that instead of manually dealing with the bill, the user just sends a photo of it in the chat.  
4. **Step 1 (Bill Capture)** Demonstrates the ease of the workflow, with the bill being understood directly from the photo without requiring any manual data entry.  
5. **Step 2 (Payment Flow)** Shows the task being completed in just a few taps, emphasizing speed and convenience rather than the payment process itself.  
6. **Step 3 (Future Reminder)** Expands the value beyond a one-time payment by showing that the system remembers the bill and will remind the user again next month.  
7. **Payoff** Reinforces the core benefit: no more forgotten due dates, no repetitive effort, and less dependence on someone else to keep track of bills.  
8. **Closer** Ends with a light, self-aware joke about finally being early for a bill payment, leaving the audience with a memorable and relatable takeaway.
