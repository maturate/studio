**Reference Trial Script  \-**   
Things you can do on WhatsApp that nobody tells you about, part 1\.  
Did you know if you go to this website, you can just click "Calorie Tracking" and it opens straight into WhatsApp? There we go. Then just type in, "had butter chicken and rice for dinner," and it comes right back with the calories, protein, the whole breakdown. Don't feel like typing? **Structure \-** 

1. **Hook** \- Opens with a curiosity-driven promise, positioning the tool as a useful WhatsApp discovery that viewers will want to see for themselves.  
2. **Setup** \- Introduces a simple website with a single button that takes users directly into a WhatsApp chat, making the process feel quick and frictionless.  
3. **Transition** \- A brief visual jump from the website to WhatsApp that delivers the reveal and smoothly moves into the demonstration.  
4. **Demo 1 (Text Logging)** \- Shows the most straightforward use case: sending a meal as a message and instantly receiving a structured nutritional breakdown.  
5. **Demo 2 (Photo Logging)** \- Highlights an even easier option by allowing users to send a photo of their food instead of typing everything out.  
6. **Demo 3 (Asking Questions)** \- Expands the use case beyond meal logging by showing how users can ask broader questions, such as checking their weekly progress.  
7. **Payoff** \- Reinforces the main benefit: everything happens inside WhatsApp, removing the need for a separate app or manual tracking.  
8. **Closer** \- Ends with a simple "Easy," giving the video a casual, confident finish that feels more like a recommendation than a pitch.

3. **Transition** \- No separate visual transition beat here; the send-and-response happens as one continuous motion, folding directly into the demo.  
4. **Demo (Photo Logging)** \- Shows the core use case: sending a photo gets a full breakdown (protein, carbs, calories) plus a qualitative read ("solid lunch, higher protein than I expected") \- combining Demo 2's photo-logging mechanic from part 1 with an unprompted insight layer.

6. **Closer** \- Ends with a forward-looking, low-key line ("gonna know exactly where I stand by tonight") instead of "Easy" \- same confident, unforced tone, but pointing ahead rather than summarizing.
